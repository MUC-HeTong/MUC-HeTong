## About Us | 关于我们

HeTong is an open research initiative dedicated to multilingual and multimodal foundation models, low-resource language technologies, cultural understanding, and trustworthy artificial intelligence.

和同（HeTong）取意于“和而不同、美美与共”，致力于构建面向低资源多语种、多模态理解、文化理解与可信人工智能的开放模型与评测生态。

## Our Projects | 开源项目

- **HeTong Models** — 多语种多模态基础模型家族
- **HeTongEval** — 开放评测与动态监测平台
- **HeTongBench** — 多语种多模态评测基准
- **HeTongRank** — 模型能力排行榜
- **HeTongReport** — 模型评测报告

## Research Directions | 研究方向

- Multilingual Large Language Models
- Low-Resource Language Technologies
- Multimodal Foundation Models
- Cultural Understanding and Alignment
- Trustworthy Artificial Intelligence
