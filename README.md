# HeTong

## HeTong Model Family

*Harmony in Diversity, Intelligence Across Languages.*

---

## About Us

HeTong is an open research initiative dedicated to multilingual and multimodal foundation models, low-resource language technologies, cultural understanding, and trustworthy artificial intelligence.

## Our Projects

- **[HeTong Models](https://github.com/MUC-HeTong/HeTong)** — Multilingual and multimodal foundation model family
- **[HeTongEval](https://github.com/MUC-HeTong/HeTongEval)** — Open evaluation and monitoring platform (under development)
- **[HeTongBench](https://github.com/MUC-HeTong/HeTongBench)** — Multilingual and multimodal evaluation benchmarks (planned)
- **[HeTongRank](https://github.com/MUC-HeTong/HeTongRank)** — Model capability leaderboards (planned)
- **HeTongReport** — Model evaluation reports (planned)

## Research Directions

- Multilingual Large Language Models
- Low-Resource Language Technologies
- Multimodal Foundation Models
- Cultural Understanding and Alignment
- Trustworthy Artificial Intelligence
